Who is this course designed for?
This course is specially designed for young adults and students aged 17 to 26 who want to learn spoken English from scratch. No prior English knowledge is required! It's perfect for college students, job seekers, and working professionals.
Do I need any special equipment or software?
No special equipment needed! Just a smartphone or computer with a good internet connection. Classes are conducted online and all materials are provided digitally.
Are the classes taught in Hindi or English?
We use a bilingual approach! Explanations and instructions are given in Hindi to ensure full understanding, while gradually transitioning students to think and speak in English. This makes learning much easier and less intimidating, especially for Hindi-medium students entering college or the job market.
What if I miss a class?
No worries! All classes are recorded and you get lifetime access to the recordings. You can catch up at any time, at your own pace — even after work or college hours.

Yes! The Certificate of Completion from Santosh Yadav English Academy is awarded upon finishing the full 21-day program. It's a great addition to your resume, LinkedIn profile, and a proud achievement for your career journey.
Is this suitable for working professionals?
Absolutely! Classes are scheduled in the evening/weekend so working professionals can attend without disrupting their routine. Many of our students are freshers, BPO aspirants, and college students who improved their English while managing their full schedule.
